Terms & Production Guidelines (制作規約・承認規定)

Ref No: MASG-2026-8812
Appendix Sheet (Page 2 / 2)

1. Production Milestones & Revision Policy

1.1. Production progresses through three mandatory approval milestones:

1. Stage 1 (Massing & Composition): Camera Angle & Clay Massing Sign-off (Clay Renderings or model check)

2. Stage 2 (Look & Atmosphere): Material, Texture & Lighting Tone Review (First Render or First Stage)

3. Stage 3 (Final Polish): High-Res Final Delivery & Retouching (Final Render)

1.2. Each project phase includes up to two (2) rounds of standard revisions within the base fee, covering the following scope:

1. Stage 1 (Massing & Composition): Corrections to 3D modeling accuracy, minor architectural adjustments, camera placement/field of view fine-tuning, and primary furniture/prop layout placement.

2. Stage 2 (Look & Atmosphere): Adjustments to material finishes, surface textures, lighting mood/time of day, entourage positioning (3D people/landscaping), and initial color grading.

3. Stage 3 (Final Polish): Minor 2D post-production enhancements, subtle color calibration, and final high-resolution artifact cleanup.

1.3. Rollbacks to previous approved milestones (e.g., changing camera angle after sign-off, or architectural design changes post-material approval) incur additional fees (from ¥30,000/round) and delivery schedule adjustments.

1.4. Substantial 3D remodeling due to architectural design modifications beyond the initial drawings will be charged (30% of CG production item cost).

2. Provided Data & Background Photo Compositing

2.1. Any modeling discrepancies, design resolution, or dimensional fixing originating from client-provided 3D (3dsmax, Revit, Rhino, SketchUp) or 2D CAD files will be evaluated and estimated based on required hours.

2.2. When compositing with site photographs or drone photography, high-resolution source photography with identified focal parameters and capture time must be provided. if not will be charged (+20,000 JPY)

3. Product-Specific Terms & Conditions

3.1. Still Images & Photomatch: Final deliverables are rendered at the contracted resolution (Standard 2K / High 4K). Any modification to camera angle, aspect ratio, or lens focal length requested after Stage 1 sign-off will be treated as an additional view and billed accordingly.

3.2. 360 VR & Before/After VR: Panoramic viewpoints and camera nodes are permanently fixed upon Stage 1 approval. Subsequent additions of node locations, camera path alterations, or interactive hotspot features outside the initial agreement require a supplementary quote.

3.3. Animation (Walkthrough & Cinematic): Camera animatics, motion speed, and timing storyboards must be formally approved prior to sequence rendering. Any changes to camera trajectory, pacing, or clip duration requested after render kickoff will incur re-rendering fees based on affected frame counts.

3.4. Shapespark Virtual Walkthrough: Web and mobile real-time performance requires strict polygon count and texture budget constraints. High-density custom 3D assets provided by the client will undergo automatic geometry optimization unless dedicated custom optimization services are added.

4. Delivery Schedule Policy

4.1. Delivery schedules are indicative and subject to adjustment based on the final agreed scope of work and project complexity within the official quotation.
